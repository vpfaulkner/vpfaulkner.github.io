Let's build something

Simple Objects is a remote software development shop specializing in AI, web and mobile applications. We help startups launch their first platform and enable enterprises to expand their digital capabilities. With deep technical expertise and a pragmatic approach, we build scalable, cutting-edge products designed for real-world impact.

Our work

Here are a few recent projects we're particularly proud of

Fintech Lender Platform

Worked with a fintech startup to build and launch an income analysis platform for lenders. Features a serverless Python backend capable analyzing thousands of tax documents per minute. Startup is growing and near acquisition.

Restaurant Revenue Forecasting App

Worked with a startup to build and launch a revenue forecasting platform for restaurants. Integrated with major POS systems and other relevant data sources to train and deploy an ML model. Helped startup hire full time dev team. Startup was later acquired.

Construction Blueprint Analysis Application

Worked with a startup to build and launch an application capable of analyzing and annotating construction blueprints for service providers. Performant AI backend processed massive construction files in minutes.

AI Technician

Worked with an established company to build an LLM-based chatbot to act as AI Technician. Diagnostic AI was powered by state-of-the-art LLM models and validated using a robust eval system with hundreds of test scenarios.

Mobile App for Smart Lamp

Build an iOS and Android app to control a smart lamp via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). Collaborated with China-based manufacturer for hardware integration and firmware over-the-air updates. Shipped product in time for Christmas release.

Internal Supply Chain Management

Built internal ingredient sourcing tool for major meal kit supplier. Worked with supply team to replace legacy system with a modern platform that dramatically reduced fulfillment time and human error.

Services

We offer end-to-end development and consulting to turn ideas into scalable, production-ready applications.

  • Application development. We build AI, web, and mobile applications from the ground up, ensuring they are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with business goals. Whether launching an MVP or expanding an existing product, we deliver robust, high-performance solutions.
  • AI/ML & Data Engineering. We design and deploy machine learning models, including LLMs, and data pipelines that power intelligent applications. Our expertise spans LLMs, traditional ML models and rules-based AI, driving real-world business impact.
  • CTO Leadership & Technical Strategy. Need an experienced hand to guide technical decisions? We provide fractional CTO services, helping startups define their tech roadmap, scale their teams, and make the right architectural choices.
  • Product Strategy & Technical Advisory. Technology is only as good as the business strategy behind it. We help align technical decisions with business objectives, ensuring your product is positioned for success.
