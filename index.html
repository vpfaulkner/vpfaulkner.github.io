Let's build something

Simple Objects is a remote software development shop specializing in AI, web and mobile applications. We help startups launch their first platform and enable enterprises to expand their digital capabilities. With deep technical expertise and a pragmatic approach, we build scalable, cutting-edge products designed for real-world impact.

Our work

Here are a few recent projects we're particularly proud of

Fintech Income Analysis Platform

Worked with a fintech startup to build and launch an income analysis platform for lenders. App consisted of a React frontend, Ruby on Rails backend, and Python AI service to extract relevant data from tax files. Startup is growing and near acquisition.

Restaurant Forecasting App

Worked with a startup to build and launch a revenue forecasting platform for restaurants. Integrated with major POS systems and other relevant data sources to train and deploy an ML model. Helped startup hire full time dev team and startup was later acquired.

Construction Blueprint Analysis

Worked with a startup to build and launch a construction blueprint platform for project bids. Performant AI backend analyzed massive construction files in minutes.

AI Technician

Worked with an established company to build an LLM-based chatbot to diagnose appliance issues. Diagnostic AI was powered by frontier LLM models and validated using a robust evaluation system with hundreds of test scenarios.

Mobile App for Smart Lamp

iOS and Android app to control a smart lamp via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) protocol. Collaborated with China-based manufacturer for hardware interation and firmware over-the-air updates. Shipped product in time for Christmas release

Internal Supply Chain Management

Built internal ingredient sourcing tool for major meal kit supplier. Designed and launched that app internally in tight 3 month timeline.

Services - Services

We offer end-to-end development and consulting to turn ideas into scalable, production-ready applications.

  • Application development. We build AI, web, and mobile applications from the ground up, ensuring they are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with business goals. Whether launching an MVP or expanding an existing product, we deliver robust, high-performance solutions.
  • AI/ML & Data Engineering. We design and deploy machine learning models and data pipelines that power intelligent applications. Our expertise includes LLMs, NLP, predictive analytics, and data transformation for real-world business impact.
  • CTO Leadership & Technical Strategy. Need an experienced hand to guide technical decisions? We provide fractional CTO services, helping startups define their tech roadmap, scale their teams, and make the right architectural choices.
  • Product Strategy & Technical Advisory. Technology is only as good as the business strategy behind it. We help align technical decisions with business objectives, ensuring your product is positioned for success.
